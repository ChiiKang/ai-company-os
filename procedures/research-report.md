# Research report procedure

Store the report only in the private knowledge root. Put the mandatory private-artifact HTML marker defined by the storage library in the first five lines, so the public-safety gate rejects an accidentally staged body. Metadata belongs in `claims-evidence` and `decision-report` artifacts; the body is Markdown and must include Mermaid.

## Evidence collection

- Establish the present date and a bounded search plan.
- Prefer official standards/docs, original repositories, datasets, regulatory material, and peer-reviewed/original papers.
- Capture title, author/publisher, publication date, retrieval date/time, URI, primary/secondary label, relevant observation, and content hash.
- Search for newer versions, corrections, retractions, disagreements, and evidence that would falsify the likely answer.
- Never use a citation as decoration: connect it to a claim and state limits.

## Progressive report structure

1. **Answer in one page (client/CEO):** answer, why it matters, recommended decision, confidence, biggest uncertainty.
2. **Decision brief (PM/CTO):** choices, trade-offs, architecture/operations, evidence quality, costs, risks.
3. **Teaching guide (junior developer):** vocabulary, fundamentals, step-by-step mechanism, example, common misconceptions and failure modes.
4. **Evidence and debate:** claim table, strongest support, strongest counter-evidence, primary-source coverage, dates.
5. **What I could not find:** missing, inaccessible, stale, disputed, or non-existent evidence and how that limits the answer.
6. **What would change this conclusion:** specific future observations, disconfirming results, or stronger sources that could reverse the decision.
7. **Sources:** complete dated citations.

Include a Mermaid diagram that explains the mechanism, evidence chain, or decision. Add generated images only when spatial/visual content cannot be explained as well by text or Mermaid; record provenance and approval for any paid generation.
