# One-batch research intake

Ask these questions together once at the beginning. This is one bounded questionnaire, not a conversational interview loop.

1. What decision or understanding should this research enable?
2. Who will use it (junior developer, product manager, CTO, CEO, client, or mixed audience)?
3. What is in scope and explicitly out of scope?
4. What metric, target, or acceptance criteria make the report useful?
5. What is the recency horizon, geography, industry, and relevant technical context?
6. Which prior beliefs, known sources, constraints, or competing explanations must be examined?
7. What output depth and deadline apply within the approved resource policy?

Combine answers with the assignment. For unanswered non-safety questions, state conservative assumptions and proceed. Do not ask the same question again or serially request preferences. Ask again only when continuing would cross an approval/safety boundary or when a genuinely missing fact makes the measurable goal impossible; stop as `approval-boundary` or `proven-blocker` rather than conducting an open-ended interview.
