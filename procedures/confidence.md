# Observable confidence rubric

Confidence is an evidence classification, not a feeling or unsupported score. Every decision and handoff records its evidence basis, major assumptions, known contradictions, alternatives tested, reversal risk, rationale, score, and evidence references.

## Low

Use **low** when any material evidence is missing, evidence is only indirect, or an outcome-reversing contradiction remains unresolved. A numeric score below 0.5 does not cure missing evidence; it only summarizes the observable basis.

## Medium

Use **medium** only when relevant primary evidence or an independently reproducible result supports the conclusion, major assumptions are named, and known contradictions do not overturn it. Scores are 0.5 up to (but not including) 0.8.

## High

Use **high** only when multiple independent evidence paths or a faithful reproduction support the result, meaningful alternatives/disconfirming explanations were tested, and remaining uncertainty is unlikely to reverse the decision. Scores are at least 0.8.

## Handoff gate

The overall handoff and **every load-bearing claim** must be medium or high. Each load-bearing claim names its evidence references and rationale. Low claims, generic confidence prose, a numeric self-score without evidence basis, or unresolved outcome-reversing contradictions stop automatic composition. Evidence provenance and uncertainty cross the handoff unchanged.
